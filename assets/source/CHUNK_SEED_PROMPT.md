## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: Minecraft 1.20.1 inventory item texture for an original mod, square transparent PNG

Primary request: one small magical 'Chunk Seed' that a player uses to reveal a new piece of world. A compact dark earth-and-stone cubelet with a single bright emerald-green sprout/crystal emerging from its top, faint warm gold seam on one side.

Style/medium: strict 16x16-style Minecraft pixel art, hand-placed-looking square pixels, no outlines thinner than one logical pixel, no smooth gradients, no anti-aliasing, no perspective beyond a simple 3/4 item silhouette.

Composition/framing: one centered item filling roughly 75% of square canvas, generous genuinely transparent padding; readable when rendered at 16x16 GUI size.

Color palette: deep slate stone, moss green, restrained emerald green, tiny warm gold accent; not blue or purple.

Constraints: transparent background with alpha, exactly one item, no text, no border, no background scene, no shadow outside sprite, no logos, no watermark.
